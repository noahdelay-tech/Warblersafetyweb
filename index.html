Read the video transcript
  1. Upload the CSV or Excel prescription file your online DTC pharmacy already has.
  2. Warbler identifies patients at risk of not renewing their prescription.
  3. Advanced voice AI calls at a time the patient previously specified and learns what is stopping renewal: shipping, insurance coverage, side effects, or education gaps.
  4. During the same call, provide personalized support based on your pharmacy’s offerings and approved information. Route side effects and other clinical concerns to your internal pharmacist or physician.
  5. Connect pharmacist calendars to automatically schedule follow-up calls around patient and pharmacist availability.
  6. Help remove barriers to the next refill, with the goal of improving adherence and focusing clinical time where it matters.
